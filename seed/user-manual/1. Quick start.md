# Welcome to Deployable Knowledge

Deployable Knowledge is a private research workbench that runs on your own computer. You give it documents, ask questions about them in a chat, and collect what you learn in notebooks. Nothing leaves your machine unless you connect an online model yourself.

> **This notebook is your user manual.** Each page covers one topic, in order. Click **Back to pages** at the top of the Notebook window to see every page, or use **Search pages...** to find a word anywhere in the manual. Press **Ctrl+F** to find a word on the current page.

# 1. Quick start

Follow these six steps once and you will have used every main part of the app.

1. **Get a model.** At the bottom-right of the **Assistant Chat** window, click the model name (it says **Select a model** the first time). Under **Local models**, click the download entry for **Gemma 4 E2B** (needs about 8 GB of memory) or **Gemma 4 E4B** (about 16 GB). Wait for the percentage to reach 100%, then click the model to select it.
2. **Add a document.** In the **Document Library** window, click **Add to corpus** at the bottom, then **Add files**, and pick a PDF, Word file or text file.
3. **Wait for it to finish.** A progress bar shows the document being read and then "Embedding X of Y chunks". When the bar disappears, the document is ready.
4. **Ask a question.** Click in the chat box ("Type a message…"), type a question about your document, and press **Enter**.
5. **Check the source.** Under the answer you will see the passages it used. Click **Open source** (the arrow icon) to see the original page.
6. **Keep the answer.** First make a notebook of your own: in the **Notebook** window click **Back to pages**, then **Back to notebooks**, click **+** (**Create notebook**), give it a name and click **Save**. Then click **Send to notebook** (the book icon) under the answer. It is added to the bottom of your new notebook's page.

> **Watch out:** **Send to notebook** always adds to the page that is open in the Notebook window. If a manual page is open, the answer lands at the bottom of that manual page. Keep your own work in your own notebooks.
