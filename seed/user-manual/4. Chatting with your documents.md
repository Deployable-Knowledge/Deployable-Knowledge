# 4. Chatting with your documents

You ask questions in the **Assistant Chat** window. Past conversations are kept in the **Chat History** window.

## What's in the Assistant Chat window

**Goals bar**

Only appears at the top of the window when the **Goal tracking** tool is in use (see **5. Tools**). It shows "Goals · 2/5" and the current goal; click it to see the whole checklist.

**The conversation**

A new chat shows "Start a conversation". After that, your messages and the assistant's answers appear here. Each answer is laid out from top to bottom:
1. **Activity rows**: collapsible rows such as **Thought process** or "Searched …". Click one to see what the model did.
2. **The answer** itself.
3. **Sources**: the passages the answer used. Each shows a type icon, the document's title and page, a preview, and two buttons on the right:
   - **Open source** (arrow out of a box): opens the original document at that spot.
   - **Save chunk** (bookmark with a +): attaches the passage to the open notebook as a source for citations, without adding any text.

   Only five sources are shown at first; click **Show N more sources** to see the rest.
4. **Footer**: on the left, counts of the tools, context and turns used (shown when tools were used). On the right, **Send to notebook** (book icon) adds the answer to the open notebook page.

While an answer is being written you will see "Thinking…", "Starting tools…" or "Writing response…".

**The chat box**
1. **Message box** ("Type a message…"): type your question here.
2. Bottom left, left to right:
   - **Chat modes** (the icon of the current mode): choose **Notebook**, **Search** or **Model**. See the table below.
   - **Tools** (wrench): turns tools on or off (see **5. Tools**). A wrench with a slash through it means tools are off. Hover over it to see what it does.
   - **New chat** (speech bubble with a +): starts a new conversation. The current one stays in Chat History.
   - **Reasoning effort** (brain icon and level): how hard the model thinks: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Extra High**. Higher is slower but more careful.
3. Bottom right, left to right:
   - **Model picker** (the model's name): choose which model answers (see **6. Models**).
   - **Send** (up arrow): sends your message. While an answer is being written it becomes **Stop generation** (square).

**Chat modes**

Only one mode is on at a time.

| Mode | Icon | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Notebook** | Open book | Uses the open notebook instead of your documents as context |
| **Search** (default) | Magnifying glass | Searches your documents before every reply |
| **Model** | Robot | The model answers on its own, without your documents, the notebook or tools |

**Tools** can be turned on or off in **Notebook** and **Search**. In **Model** it is greyed out and shows a wrench with a slash through it; hover over it for a reminder to choose **Notebook** or **Search** first.

## What's in the Chat History window

Your conversations are listed with the most recently used at the top. Each row shows, left to right:
1. **Title and date**: the chat's name and when it was last used. Click it to reopen the chat. New chats are named automatically from your first message.
2. **Chat actions** (⋯): **Rename** or **Delete** the chat.

## How to

**Ask a question**
1. Click in the message box and type your question.
2. Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift+Enter** to start a new line instead.
3. To stop an answer early, click **Stop generation** (square).

**Start a new chat**
1. Click **New chat** (speech bubble with a +) next to the chat box.

**Reopen, rename or delete a chat**
1. In **Chat History**, click a chat to reopen it.
2. To rename or delete it, click **⋯** on its row and choose **Rename** or **Delete**. Deleting cannot be undone.
