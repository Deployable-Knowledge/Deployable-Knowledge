# Welcome to Deployable Knowledge

Deployable Knowledge is a private research workbench that runs on your own computer. You give it documents, ask questions about them in a chat, and collect what you learn in notebooks.

> **This is your user guide.** Click a chapter on the left to open it, or use **Previous** and **Next** at the bottom right to read in order. Open it again at any time with the **User Guide** button (the book icon at the top right, next to **Settings**).

# 1. Quick start

Follow these seven steps once and you will have used every main part of the app.

1. **Get a model.** At the bottom-right of the **Assistant Chat** window, click the model name (it says **Select a model** the first time). Under **Local models**, click the download entry for **Gemma 4 E2B** (needs about 8 GB of memory) or **Gemma 4 E4B** (about 16 GB). Wait for the percentage to reach 100%, then click the model to select it.
2. **Add a document.** In the **Document Library** window, click **Add to corpus** at the bottom, then **Add files**, and pick a PDF, Word file or text file.
3. **Wait for it to finish.** A progress bar shows the document being read and then "Embedding X of Y chunks". When the bar disappears, the document is ready.
4. **Search your document.** In the **Search Context** window, type a few words into **Enter search text…** and press **Enter**. The passages that match best appear as cards, best first. Click **Semantic**, **Lexical** or **Hybrid** to compare the search methods (see **5. Search and Retrieval**).
5. **Ask a question.** Click in the chat box ("Type a message…"), type a question about your document, and press **Enter**.
6. **Check the source.** Under the answer you will see the passages it used. Click **Open source** (the arrow icon) to see the original page.
7. **Keep the answer.** First make a notebook: in the **Notebook** window click **+** (**Create notebook**), give it a name and click **Save**. Then click **Send to notebook** (the book icon) under the answer. It is added to the bottom of the open page.

> **Watch out:** **Send to notebook** always adds to the page that is open in the Notebook window, so open the page you want first.
