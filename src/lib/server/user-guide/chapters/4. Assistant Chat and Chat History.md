# 4. Assistant Chat and Chat History

You ask questions in the **Assistant Chat** window. Past conversations are kept in the **Chat History** window.

## What's in the Assistant Chat window

A new chat shows "Start a conversation" above the chat box. Your messages and the assistant's answers appear once you send your first question.

**The chat box**
1. **Message box** ("Type a message…"): type your question here.
2. Bottom left, left to right:
   - **Chat modes** (the icon of the current mode): choose **Notebook**, **Search** or **Model**. See the table below.
   - **Tools** (wrench): turns tools on or off (see **8. Settings**). A wrench with a slash through it means tools are off. Hover over it to see what it does.
   - **New chat** (speech bubble with a +): starts a new conversation. The current one stays in Chat History.
   - **Reasoning effort** (brain icon and level): how hard the model thinks: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Extra High**. Higher is slower but more careful.
   - **Notebook: _name_** (Notebook mode only): the notebook the chat is using. It reads every page of it.
3. Bottom right, left to right:
   - **Model picker** (the model's name, or **Select a model** if none is chosen yet): choose which model answers. While a model downloads, it also shows the download percentage. Click it to open the list, from top to bottom:
     - **Local models**: models that run inside the app, fully offline. Click a downloaded model to use it. A model not downloaded yet shows "Needs ~8 GiB RAM" and a download icon; click it to start the download. A model downloading shows "Downloading… 40%" and a progress bar.
     - **Other groups**, such as **Ollama** or a provider you've added. A group only appears when it has at least one model.
     - **Manage models** (gear icon): opens **Settings → Models** (see **8. Settings**).

     If the chosen model can't use tools, the **Tools** button is greyed out.
   - **Send** (up arrow): sends your message. While an answer is being written it becomes **Stop generation** (square).

**Chat modes**

Only one mode is on at a time.

| Mode | Icon | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Notebook** | Open book | Uses the notebook named next to **Reasoning effort** instead of your documents as context |
| **Search** (default) | Magnifying glass | Searches your documents before every reply |
| **Model** | Robot | A plain chat with the model: it answers from its own knowledge and the conversation so far, without your documents, the notebook or tools |

In every mode, the assistant remembers the conversation: the last 20 messages of the current chat are sent along with each question, so you can ask follow-ups such as "Explain that more simply." Start a **New chat** to begin with a clean slate.

**Tools** can be turned on or off in **Notebook** and **Search**. In **Model** it is greyed out and shows a wrench with a slash through it; hover over it for a reminder to choose **Notebook** or **Search** first. In **Search** mode with **Tools** on and **Document search** allowed, the model runs its own searches instead of one search before every reply: it chooses the method and reads neighbouring passages as it needs.

**The conversation**

After you send a question, your messages and the assistant's answers appear above the chat box. While an answer is being written you will see "Thinking…", "Starting tools…" or "Writing response…". Each answer is laid out from top to bottom:
1. **Activity rows**: collapsible rows such as **Thought process** or "Searched …". Click one to see what the model did.
2. **Task list**: only shown when the **Goal tracking** tool is in use (see **8. Settings**). It shows how many tasks are done, such as "2/5", and the current task; click it to see the whole checklist.
3. **The answer** itself. Charts made with the **Python** tool appear as images in the answer.
4. **Sources**: the passages the answer used. Each shows a type icon, the document's title and page, a preview, and two buttons on the right:
   - **Open source** (arrow out of a box): opens the original document at that spot.
   - **Save chunk** (bookmark with a +): attaches the passage to the open notebook as a source for citations, without adding any text.

   Only five sources are shown at first; click **Show N more sources** to see the rest.
5. **Footer**: on the left, counts of the tools, context and turns used (shown when tools were used). On the right, **Send to notebook** (book icon) adds the answer to the open notebook page (see **Send an answer to the notebook** below).

## What's in the Chat History window

Your conversations are listed with the most recently used at the top. Each row shows, left to right:
1. **Title and date**: the chat's name and when it was last used. Click it to reopen the chat. New chats are named automatically from your first message.
2. **Chat actions** (⋯): **Rename** or **Delete** the chat.

## How to

**Ask a question**
1. Click in the message box and type your question.
2. Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift+Enter** to start a new line instead.
3. To stop an answer early, click **Stop generation** (square).

**Send an answer to the notebook**
1. In the **Notebook** window, open the page you want the answer on. If you don't have a notebook yet, click **+** (**Create notebook**) first.
2. In the chat, click **Send to notebook** (book icon) at the bottom right of the answer.
3. The answer is added to the **bottom of the open page**, and the passages it used are added to the notebook's **Loaded sources** so you can cite them. A message confirms "Sent to notebook with N sources".

If no notebook page is open you'll see "Open a notebook page first."

**Start a new chat**
1. Click **New chat** (speech bubble with a +) next to the chat box.

**Reopen, rename or delete a chat**
1. In **Chat History**, click a chat to reopen it.
2. To rename or delete it, click **⋯** on its row and choose **Rename** or **Delete**. Deleting cannot be undone.
