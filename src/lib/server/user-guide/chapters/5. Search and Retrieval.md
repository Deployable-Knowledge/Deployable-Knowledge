# 5. Search and Retrieval

Search doesn't read whole documents. It works on short passages called **chunks**, and returns the chunks that best match your search text, best first. Finding those chunks is called **retrieval**.

## How search works

1. **Chunking** (when a document is added): the text is split into sentences, and the sentences are grouped into chunks of up to about 1,200 characters. Each chunk starts with the last sentence of the one before, so a passage that falls across a boundary isn't lost. Every chunk remembers where it came from: its page, sheet or place in a recording.
2. **Indexing** (also when a document is added): each chunk is added to a **keyword index**, and the **embedding model** (see **8. Settings**) turns it into an **embedding**, a list of numbers that captures its meaning. This is the "Embedding 12 of 40 chunks" step you see while a document loads.
3. **Matching** (when you search): your search text is compared with the chunks of every **active** document, using the method you choose (below).
4. **Ranking**: each matching chunk gets a score, and the highest-scoring chunks are returned, up to the number you asked for.

Only **active** documents are searched. A document you deactivate with its power button in the Document Library is ignored until you activate it again (see **3. The Document Library**).

## The three methods

**Lexical** (keyword search)

Your search text is split into words, ignoring capitals and punctuation, and the keyword index finds every chunk containing at least one of them. Chunks are ranked with a formula called **BM25**: a chunk scores higher when it contains more of your words, contains them more often, and when those words are rare in your library. A word that appears in nearly every document counts for very little.

**Semantic** (meaning search)

Your search text is turned into an embedding by the same embedding model, and compared with the embedding of every chunk. The chunks whose meaning is closest score highest, even if they share no words with your search: "stop the bleeding" can find a chunk about "applying a tourniquet".

**Hybrid** (default)

Runs a Lexical and a Semantic search at the same time, each collecting twice as many chunks as you asked for. The two lists are combined and duplicates removed. A **re-ranking model** then reads your search text together with each chunk and scores from 0 to 1 how well the chunk answers it. The highest-scoring chunks are kept. This is slower than either method alone, but the most accurate.

| Method | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Lexical** | Names, codes, part numbers and exact phrases |
| **Semantic** | Questions in your own words and ideas that can be worded many ways |
| **Hybrid** | Almost everything; the best starting point |

To change the retrieval method and the number of retrieved chunks, go to **Settings → Agent → Retrieval** (see **8. Settings**).

> **Good to know:**
> - Lexical search matches whole words only: "pump" doesn't find "pumps".
> - CSV files are only added to the keyword index, so they are found by Lexical search, or the keyword half of Hybrid.
> - A document that is still being embedded can already be found by Lexical search, but not yet by Semantic search.

## What's in the Search Context window

The **Search Context** window lets you run a search yourself and see exactly which chunks come back.

**Running a search**
1. **Search box** ("Enter search text…"): what to search for.
2. **Number box**: how many results to show.
3. **Search** (magnifying glass): runs the search.

**Comparing methods**
1. **Semantic / Lexical / Hybrid**: switch between methods to compare their results. The results change instantly; there's no need to search again.

**Results**

Each result card shows, from top to bottom:
1. Its rank (**#1**, **#2**…), the document's title, and where the chunk is (**Page 4**, **Sheet 2** or **Transcript**).
2. The full text of the chunk.
3. Buttons, left to right:
   - **Save chunk** (bookmark with a +): attaches the chunk to the open notebook as a source for citations.
   - **Send to notebook** (book icon): adds the chunk's text to the open notebook page.
   - **Open document** (arrow out of a box), or **Play this chunk** for audio: opens the original at that spot.

Changes you make in this window don't alter your settings.

## How to

**Search your documents**
1. In **Search Context**, type in **Enter search text…**.
2. Optionally change the number next to it.
3. Click **Search** or press **Enter**.
4. Click **Semantic**, **Lexical** or **Hybrid** to compare the results.
