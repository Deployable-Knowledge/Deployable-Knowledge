# 6. The Notebook

The **Notebook** window is where you write notes and keep answers. It is organized as **notebooks → pages**, and shows one of three views: the list of notebooks, the pages in a notebook, or the page you're editing. Use the back arrow at the top left to move up a level.

## What's in the notebooks list

**Adding notebooks**
1. **Notebooks**: the view's title.
2. **Create notebook** (+): makes a new notebook.
3. **Import notebook from folder** (upload icon): turns a folder of Markdown or text files into a new notebook, one page per file.

**Finding and opening notebooks**
1. **Search notebooks...**: finds text in every notebook and page.
2. **The notebooks**, each showing, left to right:
   - **Grip** (dotted handle): drag it to reorder the notebooks.
   - **Book icon and title**: click to open the notebook.
   - **⋯**: **Rename**, **Export as ZIP** or **Delete**.

## What's in a notebook's page list

**Moving around and adding pages**
1. **Back to notebooks** (back arrow).
2. **The notebook's title**: click it to rename the notebook.
3. **Create page** (+): adds a new page.
4. **Import Markdown or text file** (upload icon): adds files as new pages.

**Finding and opening pages**
1. **Search pages...**: finds text in this notebook's pages.
2. **The pages**, each showing, left to right:
   - **Grip** (dotted handle): drag it to reorder the pages.
   - **Page icon, title and a preview of its text**: click to open the page.
   - **⋯**: **Rename**, **Move to notebook**, **Export as Markdown** or **Delete**.

## What's in the page editor

**Page title and tools**
1. **Back to pages** (back arrow).
2. **The page's title**: click it to rename the page.
3. **Find in page** (magnifying glass, or **Ctrl+F** while your cursor is in the page): opens a find bar at the bottom right of the page.
4. **View loaded sources** (stack of files, with a count): opens the sources panel used for citations (see below).

**Formatting toolbar**

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Bold** (Ctrl+B), **Italic** (Ctrl+I), **Strikethrough** | Text style |
| **Text color**, **Highlight** | Color the selected text. Pick the same color again to remove it |
| **Heading 1–3** | Section titles |
| **Bulleted list**, **Numbered list**, **Quote** | Structure |
| **Inline code**, **Code block** | Code |
| **Link** | Insert a link |

**Writing area**

Just type; the page is in Markdown and is formatted as you write. **There is no save button: everything saves automatically.**

**Character count**
1. **Character count** (bottom right): how many characters the notebook uses across all its pages.

**Markdown cheat sheet**

You can also type the formatting yourself. The symbols hide once your cursor leaves the line; click back into a line to see them again.

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `# Heading` | A large heading. Use `##` or `###` for smaller ones |
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` | *italic* |
| `~~strikethrough~~` | ~~strikethrough~~ |
| `` `code` `` | `code` |
| `- item` | A bulleted list item |
| `1. item` | A numbered list item |
| Two spaces, then `- item` | An item nested under the one above |
| `> text` | A quote |
| `[text](https://example.com)` | A link |
| `[text]{color=red}` | [red text]{color=red} |
| `[text]{hl=yellow}` | [highlighted text]{hl=yellow} |
| `[text]{color=red hl=yellow}` | [red, highlighted text]{color=red hl=yellow} |
| `` ``` `` on its own line before and after | A code block |

Put the text in square brackets, then the style in curly brackets straight after it, with no space in between. Inside the curly brackets, `color=` sets the text color and `hl=` sets the highlight; to use both, separate them with a space.

| Use | Names |
|---|---|
| `color=` | [red]{color=red}, [orange]{color=orange}, [yellow]{color=yellow}, [green]{color=green}, [blue]{color=blue}, [violet]{color=violet}, [pink]{color=pink}, [gray]{color=gray} |
| `hl=` | [yellow]{hl=yellow}, [orange]{hl=orange}, [green]{hl=green}, [teal]{hl=teal}, [blue]{hl=blue}, [violet]{hl=violet}, [pink]{hl=pink}, [gray]{hl=gray} |

Any other name is left as plain text. The **Text color** and **Highlight** buttons in the toolbar type this for you.

To make a table, type rows like this. The second line is what turns it into a table:

```
| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| Ada | Analyst |
```

Keys for editing a table, with your cursor inside it:

| Key | In a table |
|---|---|
| **Tab** | Next cell. At the end of the last row, adds a new row |
| **Shift+Tab** | Previous cell |
| **Enter** | Cell below. On the last row, adds a new row |
| **Ctrl+Shift+K** | Deletes the current row (the header row stays) |
| **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K** | Deletes the current column (a table keeps at least one) |

Columns line up automatically when you click into a table.

> **Good to know:** checkboxes (`- [ ]`), images and horizontal lines (`---`) are shown as plain text.

## What's in the loaded sources panel

Click **View loaded sources** in the page editor to open it. From top to bottom:
1. **Loaded sources (N)**, with **Insert citations table** and **Clear all** next to it.
2. One card per source, showing:
   - The document's title and page, and **Remove source** (X) on the right.
   - A preview of the passage.
   - **Insert citation** and **Open document**.

Sources are added when you use **Send to notebook** or **Save chunk** in the chat or in Search Context.

## How to

**Create a notebook or page**
1. Go to the notebooks list, or open a notebook to see its pages.
2. Click **+** (**Create notebook** or **Create page**), type a title and click **Save**.

A new notebook starts with an empty "Page 1".

**Rename, move, export or delete**
1. Click **⋯** next to the notebook or page.
2. Choose **Rename**, **Move to notebook** (pages only), **Export as ZIP** / **Export as Markdown**, or **Delete**.

Exported files keep your text colors and highlights in a form other Markdown apps can display. Importing those files back turns them into the `[text]{color=…}` form again.

**Send a chat answer to the notebook**
1. Open the page you want the answer on.
2. In the chat, click **Send to notebook** (book icon) under the answer.
3. The answer is added to the **bottom of the open page**, and its sources are added to **Loaded sources**.

You can do the same with a passage in **Search Context**: click **Send to notebook** on a result card.

**Add a citation**
1. Click **View loaded sources** at the top of the page editor.
2. Put your cursor where the citation should go.
3. Click **Insert citation** on a source. A link like *(Report title, p. 4)* is added.
4. To list every source at the end of the page, click **Insert citations table**. It adds or updates a "Citations" table.

Click a citation link to open that page of the original document.

> **Good to know:** links in a table, like the ones in the "Citations" table, work too. Click the table once to show its text, then click the link.

**Chat about your notebook**
1. In the chat box, open **Chat modes** and choose **Notebook**.
2. Ask your question. The assistant reads **every page of the open notebook** plus its loaded sources, which is good for "Tidy up my notes" or "What's missing from this outline?".

**Add a notebook page to the Document Library**

There is no one-click button yet. Either:
- Click **⋯ → Export as Markdown** on the page, then in the **Document Library** click **Add to corpus → Add files** and pick the exported file, or
- copy the page text and use **Add to corpus → Add text**.
