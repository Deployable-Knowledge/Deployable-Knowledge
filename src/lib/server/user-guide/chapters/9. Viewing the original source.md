# 9. Viewing the original source

You can always check what an answer is based on. The original opens in a new tab.

| Source type | What opens |
|---|---|
| PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel | The document, opened at the cited page |
| Text, Markdown, CSV | The file, with the cited passage highlighted and scrolled into view |
| Audio | A transcript player. Click any passage to play from that moment; change speed with **Playback speed** |

## How to

**From a chat answer**
1. Under the answer, find the source you want.
2. Click **Open source** (arrow out of a box) on its right.

**From Search Context**
1. Click **Open document** on a result card, or **Play this chunk** for audio.

**From the Document Library**
1. Click the document's title.

**From a notebook citation**
1. Click the citation link, or **Open document** in the loaded sources panel.
